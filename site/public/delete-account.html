Delete your RideConnectX account

You can permanently delete your RideConnectX account and the data stored with it.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open RideConnectX and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm it is you: pick your Google account, or enter your password. If you signed in with an email link a while ago, the app asks you to sign in again first.

Deletion happens immediately.

Without the app

Email rideconnectx.app@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject Delete my RideConnectX account. Your account and its data will be deleted within 30 days, and you will get a confirmation.

What is deleted

  • Your account (sign-in)
  • Your profile: name, nickname, city, vehicle, photo
  • Your scooter's saved last readings
  • Finished-ride summaries stored in the cloud
  • Your recent sign-ins list
  • That account's data on the phone you delete it from

What is kept

Nothing is kept after deletion. Data that lives only on a phone (for example emergency contacts) is removed from the phone the deletion is made on; on any other phone, uninstall the app or use Delete local data.
