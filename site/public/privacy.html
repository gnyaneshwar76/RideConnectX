Privacy Policy — RideConnectX

Last updated: 17 October 2026 Contact: rideconnectx.app@gmail.com

RideConnectX ("the app") connects your phone to your scooter's instrument cluster over Bluetooth Low Energy, so the cluster can show turn-by-turn directions, a notification lamp and your name. This policy explains what the app collects, where it goes, and what stays on your phone.

This policy was written from an audit of what the code actually does. It is not legal advice.

1. The short version

  • Most of what the app knows never leaves your phone.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Notification content is read on-device and relayed to your scooter. It is never uploaded.
  • The Bluetooth link to the cluster is not encrypted: caller and sender names cross the air in plain text. Names are shown on the cluster and kept in the Notifications page on the phone. Message text is read only to count and alert; it is never stored or uploaded. A name with no Latin letters is shown as the fixed label "New message".
  • While your scooter is connected, the app records your ride with GPS (precise location). The route of a finished ride is saved on your phone. If you do not keep the ride, it is deleted automatically 30 days after it ended. Only if you sign in and tap Keep is the ride, with its route, also saved to your account, for at most 10 rides. You can delete a ride at any time (section 7). One more exception: when you look up nearby service centres, your approximate position is sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service (see section 5).
  • Guests upload nothing.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

None of this is uploaded. Each item is tied to the account that created it and is not shown to any other account signed in on the same phone:

Ride recorder file (only if you switch Settings → Ride testing → Ride recorder on): each Google Maps instruction such as "Turn left onto MG Road", the turn sent to the cluster, the Maps destination text, the caller or sender name of calls and messages (never the message text), and route start and end times. Emergency contacts are other people's details. Only add people who have agreed to be your emergency contact.

3. What is stored in your account

If you sign in, the following is saved to Firebase (Google Cloud) under your account, and security rules make it readable only by you:

  • Your name, email address and sign-in method
  • Rider name, nickname and city
  • Selected vehicle model and colour
  • Account dates (when the account was created and last signed in) and a default settings record (theme, units, voice guidance, auto-connect, map type, route avoidances, notifications). These defaults are created when you first sign in; the app does not currently change them.
  • Profile photo, if you set one, stored at a reduced size
  • Your scooter's last odometer, trip and fuel readings, saved when you sign out so they are there when you sign back in
  • Your last 10 sign-ins: time, sign-in method (Google, email and password, or email link), phone model, Android version and app version. No IP address, no location and no device ID. You can see them in Profile → Recent sign-ins, to spot a sign-in that was not you; older ones are deleted automatically. This is kept so you can check your account's security.
  • Rides you keep (maximum 10): start and end time, distance, duration, top and average speed, ride title, vehicle and the route (a list of GPS points, which is precise location history). Rides you do not keep are never uploaded. Deleting a ride in the app also deletes its cloud copy.

You can use the app as a guest, in which case nothing is uploaded at all.

4. Permissions, and exactly what each is for

Permission What the app does with it
Bluetooth / nearby devices Find and connect to your scooter's cluster. Used for nothing else.
Notification access Read the notification that Google Maps posts while navigating, so the next turn can be drawn on your cluster; and detect that a call or message arrived so the cluster's lamp lights. For calls and messages (phone dialer, WhatsApp, SMS and similar apps) the app reads the caller or sender name and, for messages, the message text, and may show them on your cluster. Content is processed on the phone and sent only to your scooter. It is never uploaded.
Location Sharing your position when you tap share on the SOS screen; filling in your city on your profile; while you navigate, comparing your GPS position with the next turn to count down the distance on the cluster (on the phone only, not stored); recording your ride route while the scooter is connected (saved on the phone; uploaded only for rides you keep while signed in); and finding nearby service centres (section 5). On Android 11 and older, Android also requires it for Bluetooth scanning. The app records a route only for the ride while the scooter is connected, and you decide whether to keep it.
Contacts Only when you tap to pick an emergency contact. The app keeps that one contact's name and number.
Photos Only when you choose a profile picture.
Phone state Only to read your mobile signal strength, which is shown as bars on the cluster. The app does not read your calls, call log or phone number.
Notifications (post) Show the ongoing notification Android requires while the app holds the Bluetooth link.

The app deliberately does not request permission to read SMS, read your call log, or place calls. Calls open your dialer with the number filled in; you press the call button.

5. Other services, and no analytics

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no advertising identifier. It uses these outside services:

  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google) store your sign-in and the account data in section 3, only when you sign in. Google Sign-In is used if you choose it. Sign-in link, verification and password-reset emails are sent from rideconnectx.app@gmail.com. Google's handling is described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass (overpass-api.de): when you pick a service centre, your position, rounded to about 1 km, and a search radius are sent to find places nearby. Like any web request, the service also sees your IP address. Nothing is linked to your account. This optional "service centres near me" lookup uses a service we do not operate. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.
  • Firebase App Check (Google) confirms to Firebase that requests come from this app, not from a copy or script. It exchanges an attestation token with Google; it carries no personal data from your profile.
  • Android Geocoder: turns coordinates into a place or city name. On most phones this is done by Google's location service, under Google's terms.

6. What is never collected

  • The text of your messages or calls. A caller or sender name is relayed to the cluster and kept in the in-app notification list on your phone; the message text is neither stored nor uploaded
  • Ride log is off by default and stays off until you switch it on.
  • A history of where you have travelled outside rides: routes are recorded only while the scooter is connected, and are uploaded only for rides you keep
  • Your address book as a whole
  • Payment information — the app takes no payments

7. Your choices

  • Use it as a guest — nothing is uploaded.
  • Sign out — your last readings are saved to your account, and your profile and readings are removed from the phone.
  • Delete local data — wipes rides, notifications, service records, emergency contacts, the paired vehicle, cached readings, the ride log, your avatar and profile preferences from the phone.
  • Delete your account — in the app: Profile → Delete account. After you confirm it is you, your cloud data (profile, photo, readings, rides), your account, and that account's data on the phone are permanently deleted, including your sign-in history. Without the app, request deletion at https://rideconnectx-89528dd3.web.app/delete-account.html.
  • Revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings. The app degrades rather than breaking: without location, SOS tells you it cannot share a position instead of inventing one.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws elsewhere, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Most of this you can do yourself in the app; for anything else, email the contact at the top. Requests are answered within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for them. It is a companion to a motor vehicle.

10. Data retention

Local data stays until you delete it, sign out, or uninstall. Account data stays until you delete your account, and is then removed immediately.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will say so before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
